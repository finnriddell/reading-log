About this log

This is my reading log: every article I read from the FT, The New Yorker, The Economist, Wired, NOEMA and others, scored from 1 to 5 on interest, importance and style, with a short takeaway written straight after reading. I keep it to read more deliberately and to sharpen my own analysis and writing.

It is also a small pool of human judgement and learning. Building it has improved my knowledge of AI tools, but it has also strengthened my appreciation for human thinking. The readings come from some of the finest minds writing today. The writing is world class; the judgement is messy and human. Together, this may become valuable when information, data and judgement are swamped by artificially generated ideas, conclusions and writing.

I designed and directed the site and coded it with Claude, an AI model. I set the rules, the scoring and the design, and I checked and tested every change. It runs for free on Google Sheets and Apps Script: a small capture page adds each article to the Sheet, and this page reads from it.

Favourites stay large as the pile ages, so the best reads are easy to find. Some takeaways are private; you'll see the ones I've chosen to share.
